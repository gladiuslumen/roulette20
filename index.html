Payouts

The wheel has 21 pockets: numbers 1–20 plus a green 0. Every bet has the same house edge of 1/21 (about 4.76%). Chips are for play only and have no cash value.

Colours. Red: 1, 3, 6, 8, 9, 11, 14, 16, 17, 19. Black: 2, 4, 5, 7, 10, 12, 13, 15, 18, 20. 0 is green and loses every bet except Zero.

How to bet. Tap a number for a straight bet. Tap the gold line between two numbers for a split, or the diamond where four numbers meet for a corner. R1–R5 and C1–C4 cover that whole row or column. Choose a chip first, then tap. Undo, Clear and Repeat work on the current layout.
